# F3: Architecture winners

## Findings

### [1] The leading public recipe (score-sorted #1 family, ~0.957) is a 4-stage multi-architecture stack: 20 DINOv2/v3 slot-attention members → five A5 attention-pooling folds at 45% → RadImageNet ResNet-50 heads → four "Raptor" CoAtNet views plus a residual CoAtNet with per-finding routing.
- quote: "Stage | Models | Blend role — 1 | 20 DINOv2/v3 slot-attention members | base ranking; 2 | five A5 attention-pooling folds | 45% against stage 1; 3 | RadImageNet ResNet-50 with E10/E13/E11 heads | reference, second pass, calibration; 4 | four Raptor CoAtNet views plus residual CoAtNet | final per-finding routing"
- url: https://www.kaggle.com/code/fuadmuhammad/rsna-knee-fast-parent-0-957-v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09 (approx; competition ongoing, 24 days to go as of 2026-09-29)
- confidence: high

### [2] The same 0.957 recipe explicitly matches the two highest score-sorted public notebooks (Evgendvorkin v14 and Mattia Angeli v32 "Bend the Knee to the Dinosaurs"), and is derived from maverickss26's public 0.941 solution.
- quote: "This version matches the public score recipe in the two highest notebooks in Kaggle's score-descending listing: Evgendvorkin's v14 and Mattia Angeli's v32. They give the MaxSpan forward/reverse views 0.60/0.10 instead of 0.55/0.15. The Native384 Dense and Native384 views remain 0.10/0.20."
- url: https://www.kaggle.com/code/fuadmuhammad/rsna-knee-fast-parent-0-957-v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09 (approx)
- confidence: high

### [3] Multi-series/multi-view fusion in the top stack is rank-weighted over four constructed views of the same study: MaxSpan forward 0.60, MaxSpan reverse 0.10, Native384 Dense 0.10, Native384 0.20, with final blend using Raptor's already-ranked [0,1] columns directly (not re-ranked to [1/N, 1]).
- quote: "adopted the top public Raptor view weights: MaxSpan forward 0.55 → 0.60 and reverse 0.15 → 0.10; Native384 Dense stayed 0.10 and Native384 stayed 0.20... matches the #1 score-sorted public recipe exactly at the final blend: Raptor's existing [0, 1] ranks are used directly instead of being reranked to [1/N, 1]."
- url: https://www.kaggle.com/code/fuadmuhammad/rsna-knee-fast-parent-0-957-v2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09 (approx)
- confidence: high

### [4] A published 0.941 public "SOTA" open notebook is DINOv2-Small + RadImageNet ResNet-50 + a meniscus specialist head, with the same four-stage shape: report-label extractor, DINO study-level member combine, A5 attention-pooling folds at 45%, then CoAtNet Raptor views with residual checkpoints and final routing.
- quote: "DinoV2 Small + RadImageNet ResNet50 + Meniscus Specialist ... Combines self-supervised vision features from DINOv2 with medical embeddings from RadImageNet and specialized residual attention blocks. Stage 2 — A5: attention-pooling folds at 45% ... Stage 4 — CoAtNet: Raptor views, residual checkpoints, final routing"
- url: https://www.kaggle.com/code/beraterolelk/0-941-sota-rsna-knee-dinov2-radimagenet
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-08 (approx; v3 of notebook)
- confidence: high

### [5] CoAtNet (specifically coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_384, i.e. CoAtNet-2 @ 384px) is the dominant strong-single-model backbone among top-150 public scorers, used at 224/288/352/384; a single-fold 2.5D CoAtNet at 224px already reaches 0.950 LB.
- quote: "Got a single fold CoAtNet at 224px to 0.950 right now... The model is pretty standard: 2.5D CoAtNet at 224 (a few neighbouring slices at a time)."
