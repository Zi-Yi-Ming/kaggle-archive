# F1: Top LB strategies discussion

## Findings

### [1] RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection 2026 is a 12-label macro-AUC code competition: 4,407 train studies / 24,371 series / ~820k slices, but only 58 studies carry expert image labels — the other 4,349 have only free-text multilingual radiology reports, and test.csv has no report column at all, so report→label extraction is the decisive problem, not the vision backbone.
- quote: "There are 4,407 training studies, 24,371 DICOM series, roughly 820,000 slices. Only 58 of those studies come with expert-assigned labels. Every other study has to be labeled from the free-text radiology report attached to it... The image model is the well-trodden 80%. A clean 12-label target for 4,349 unlabeled studies is the hard 20% that actually decides where you land."
- url: https://huggingface.co/blog/bishnoiyash/rsna-competetion
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-08-09
- confidence: high

### [2] A public leaderboard score of 0.903 macro ROC-AUC ranked 18th of 792 teams (top 2.3%) as of 2026-08-09 — i.e. the 0.93–0.96 top cluster was already well above a carefully engineered top-2.3% single-pipeline result roughly 6 weeks before the 2026-10-22 deadline.
- quote: "It produced a public leaderboard score of 0.903 macro ROC AUC, good for rank 18 of 792 teams (top 2.3%) as of the morning of August 9, 2026."
- url: https://huggingface.co/blog/bishnoiyash/rsna-competetion
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-08-09
- confidence: high

### [3] Label quality is the binding ceiling: the rank-18 pipeline used a locally hosted Qwen3.6-35B (Ollama) with a clinical-rule prompt, validated against the 58 gold studies and revised from 0.828 to 0.833 mean per-class accuracy / 0.766 mean F1 — and even a perfect report reader tops out around 0.88–0.92 because gold labels come from image re-reads that disagree with the text report.
- quote: "The revised prompt reached 0.833 mean accuracy, 0.766 mean F1, at roughly 3 seconds per report... gold labels here come from an expert re-reading the actual images, not from the text report, so even a perfect report reader has an intrinsic accuracy ceiling somewhere around 0.88 to 0.92, because a report and an image do not always agree."
- url: https://huggingface.co/blog/bishnoiyash/rsna-competetion
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-08-09
- confidence: high

### [4] A "public reference (single model)" submission already scores 0.928 public LB / 0.920 gold-58 — the closest public artifact to the 0.93–0.96 cluster found in this search — while a full CoAtNet-2 window-bag pipeline with 64 slices reaches 0.920 LB and a prior DINOv2-S 18-slice model only 0.853, showing slice coverage (18→64) matters more than swapping the encoder.
- quote: "DINOv2-S, 6 views, 18 slices: Gold-58 0.820, Public LB 0.853. CoAtNet, 64 slices, window bags: Gold-58 0.903, Holdout 0.890, Public LB 0.920. Public reference (single model): Gold-58 0.920, Public LB 0.928."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Soft report-derived labels with per-cell confidence used as loss weights beat hard 0/1 labels: hedges like "tear suspected" become ~0.8, and a finding the report never mentions contributes almost no gradient instead of being silently taught as absent (critical for synovitis, which is rarely mentioned).
- quote: "Reports are read into twelve probabilities per study rather than twelve yes/no answers: a report that hedges ('tear suspected') becomes ~0.8, not 1. Each cell also carries a confidence, which is used directly as its weight in the loss — a finding the report never discusses contributes almost no gradient instead of being silently taught as absent."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [6] Horizontal flips (aug or TTA) are a confirmed negative: 5 of 12 labels are laterality-specific (medial/lateral meniscus, medial/lateral OA, MCL), the DICOM Laterality tag is missing on half the corpus by whole vendors, and a flip swaps medial/lateral while the label stays put.
- quote: "Because of this, horizontal flips are banned from augmentation and test-time augmentation alike: a flip swaps medial and lateral... Horizontal flip (augmentation or TTA) — Swaps medial and lateral, undoing the laterality normalisation — confirmed negative elsewhere too."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [7] Blending models trained on the same report-derived labels is worthless: blending the 0.920 CoAtNet with the 0.928 public reference gave rank correlation 0.92 and no gain — "the same recipe on the same labels makes the same mistakes." Diversity must come from different label sources or different view construction, not just another backbone.
- quote: "Blending our model with the public 0.928 one — Worthless (rank correlation 0.92): the same recipe on the same labels makes the same mistakes."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] Naive equal fusion of four report-label readers is clearly worse than the best single reader — with only 58 gold studies the fusion weights cannot be estimated reliably; weighted fusion by measured per-class accuracy (as in the rank-18 pipeline) is the workable alternative, and a public third-party label set (kaggle.com/datasets/pilkwang/rsna-knee-llm-labels) is already used as one vote.
- quote: "Fusing four report-label readers equally — Clearly worse than the best single reader; with 58 studies, the fusion weights cannot be estimated."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [9] Measurement discipline is decisive because gold-58 AUC has standard error ~0.086: the 58 gold studies must never be used for training or model selection (a leaked gold58 number of 0.9357 collapsed to an honest cross-fitted 0.8568), and a change should only be adopted if it clears a measured noise floor (~0.0015 run-to-run on a 652-study holdout, so require ≥0.004 holdout gain).
- quote: "An AUC over 58 studies has a standard error near 0.086; per fold it is worse. This project's own history contains three decisions taken on differences smaller than that noise, and two leaderboard regressions that followed."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] The rank-18 winning config from a 20-trial hyperparameter search was deliberately modest — EfficientNet-B3, K=3 series, T=12 slices, plain max-pooling (not gated attention), medium aug — and a 5-fold GroupKFold model with plain mean-of-sigmoid ensemble; gated-attention MIL pooling lost to max-pool at this data scale, and larger backbones (DINOv2-B) looked better on proxy trials but worse at full scale.
- quote: "The best of those 20 trials scored 0.6811 on that blend and picked efficientnet_b3 with K=3 series, T=12 training slices, max-pooling, and medium augmentation as the winning combination... Gated attention is the more sophisticated choice and was the code's default, but the hyperparameter search... landed on plain max-pooling over slices instead."
- url: https://huggingface.co/blog/bishnoiyash/rsna-competetion
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-08-09
- confidence: high

### [11] Per-finding attention pooling (12 queries, one per abnormality) is the architectural idea most consistently associated with strong multi-label knee MRI scores in public pipelines: ACL tears live on 2–3 slices while OA spreads over many, so a single pooled embedding forces incompatible findings to share slice importance — CoAtNet-2 @384 with per-finding attention + 64-slice window bags (3 adjacent slices as RGB) reached 0.920 LB / 0.903 gold-58.
- quote: "Giving each finding its own attention weights lets each one read the slices that actually show it."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [12] Hard negative findings for top-cluster work: judging label extractors by accuracy (not AUC) wasted a week and discarded seven findings; more slices at inference than at training hurt 5/5 folds; and the weakest macro-AUC columns are laterality-dependent — lateral meniscus (−0.09 vs public reference) and MCL (−0.06) — so any 0.93→0.96 gap is likely sitting in those labels, not in overall architecture.
- quote: "Where the current model still loses against the public reference: lateral meniscus (−0.09) and MCL (−0.06), both defined by the medial/lateral compartment. Where it wins: synovitis (+0.02) and Baker's cyst (+0.02)."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [13] Competition constraints that shape every top solution: code competition, internet off, ≤9 hours GPU on ~1,300–1,322 hidden test studies; $77,000 prize pool including for the first time a model-efficiency award; entry closes 2026-10-15, final submission 2026-10-22; winners announced November and recognized at RSNA 2026 (Nov 29–Dec 3, Chicago).
- quote: "RSNA runs this as a code competition: your notebook gets attached to the hidden test set, internet access is disabled, and you get up to 9 hours of GPU time to score roughly 1,300 studies."
- url: https://huggingface.co/blog/bishnoiyash/rsna-competetion
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-08-09
- confidence: high

### [14] Independent public pipelines converge on the same preprocessing contract: sort slices by ImagePositionPatient projected on the slice normal (NOT filename / InstanceNumber — rank correlation ~0.01), per-series percentile intensity windowing (MR has no absolute scale), mm-space cropping via PixelSpacing (varies 3.4× across scanners), and no horizontal flip; one pipeline pre-decodes all 4,407 studies to a 31.8 GB uint8 memmap because DICOM decode was ~96% of epoch time.
- quote: "Slices are ordered by geometry, not by filename. DICOM filenames are SOP Instance UIDs, whose rank correlation with physical position is ~0.01."
- url: https://github.com/mathischmp/challenge_rsna
- source_type: community
- published: unknown
- confidence: high
